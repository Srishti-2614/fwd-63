Report a mess problem. Watch it get fixed.

Pick a category, describe what went wrong, and your complaint goes on a board that students and the mess committee can both see. Your name is optional.

Submit a Complaint View Board

How it works

1. Choose a category

Food quality, hygiene, staff, queues, menu or other.

2. Describe the problem

Write what happened. You can submit anonymously.

3. Check the board

See if your complaint is still Open or has been Resolved.

What you can report

Food quality

Taste, temperature, freshness.

Hygiene

Utensils, tables, kitchen and washing area.

Staff

Behaviour and serving.

Queues and timings

Long waits, late opening, early closing.

Menu

Repeated dishes, items missing from the menu.

Other

Anything else about the mess.
